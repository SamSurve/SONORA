Frequently Asked Questions

Is SONORA completely free to use?

Yes. SONORA is 100% free with no ads, no registration, and no tracking.

What is the difference between Direct Stream Copy and Transcoding?

Direct Stream Copy extracts the raw source audio stream (Opus/M4A) without re-encoding, preserving exact fidelity and downloading fastest. Transcoding converts source audio to MP3 or FLAC for specific device compatibility.

Does converting to FLAC make lossy sources lossless?

No. FLAC is a lossless container, but converting a lossy YouTube/SoundCloud stream to FLAC cannot restore frequencies already discarded by the platform. SONORA communicates quality semantics transparently.
